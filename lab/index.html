START WITH THE REQUIREMENTS
01 / THE ASSIGNMENT

The creative
brief.

A realistic, fictional commission for a local cultural venue. The design challenge is not just to look interesting; it is to let a visitor confidently choose and book a workshop.

FICTIONAL CLIENTFIELDWORK•

Workshops for curious hands.

BRIEF ID FW–26–01FORMAT Responsive landing pageMARKET London / UK · Illustrative

01The problem

A small community studio has three weekend making workshops, but prospective attendees cannot quickly discover the schedule, cost or accessibility information. The studio needs one clear page that makes booking feel approachable.

02Audience & objective

London adults aged approximately 25–40 exploring hands-on creative activities, including first-time visitors. Help them compare workshops, understand what is included, and select a clear booking action.

03Required content

The page must name the workshops, publish dates/times, show prices in GBP, identify the venue, include a working demonstration of a booking action, and state where to find accessibility information. Contact and refund wording must not imply an actual commercial booking service.

04Success definition

At a glance, a first-time visitor should be able to find a suitable workshop, understand the practical details and activate a booking route. Visual originality matters only when it supports comprehension.

DELIVERY CONSTRAINTS
  • Responsive at 375px and desktop
  • Clear semantic headings and controls
  • Readable contrast and keyboard access
  • No fabricated customer endorsements
  • No dark patterns or hidden fees
  • Friendly, contemporary voice
